About this community resource

The RAMP Navigation Hub helps people discover, understand and navigate the NHS App with confidence, whether they use a smartphone, tablet or web browser.

What the site can do

  • Explain the NHS App journey and access routes.
  • Help people use the local guides.
  • Check whether a device appears ready for app installation.
  • Identify what might be stopping progress.
  • Signpost to the appropriate source of support.

What the site and volunteers do not do

  • Change phone security settings.
  • Reset passwords, Apple accounts, Google accounts or devices.
  • Change parental controls or Screen Time settings.
  • Diagnose wider device, network or email faults.
  • Access NHS accounts, health records or clinical information.
  • Provide medical advice.

Protect your information

Do not enter or share passwords, one-time security codes, linkage keys, passphrases, identity-document images, NHS numbers or detailed medical information.

Who may be able to help?

Depending on the issue: NHS App support, your GP practice, Apple or Google support, your mobile network, your email provider, a local library or community service, or a trusted family member or friend.

Review status

The source register was prepared on 4 August 2026. NHS links, videos and guide wording should be reviewed before major publication and periodically afterwards.

RAMP Navigation Hub helps people understand and navigate the NHS App. It does not access NHS accounts or provide medical advice.
